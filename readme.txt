Comprehensive SEO add-on for NovaTools — meta management, schema, sitemaps, redirects, breadcrumbs, and more.

== Description ==

**NovaTools - SEO** is a powerful, professional, and marketing-focused SEO plugin designed specifically as an add-on for the NovaTools ecosystem. It acts as a comprehensive alternative to plugins like Yoast and RankMath, deeply integrating with both WordPress and WooCommerce to give store owners and content creators unparalleled control over their search engine visibility.

Take your website's organic traffic to the next level with advanced, automated SEO features.

> **Important:** This is an add-on plugin. It requires the base **NovaTools** plugin to be installed and activated.

### 🚀 Key Features

* **Advanced Meta Management:** Take total control over SEO titles, meta descriptions, canonical URLs, and robots meta tags (`index`, `noindex`, `follow`, `nofollow`) across all your posts, pages, and custom post types.
* **Intelligent Schema Markup (JSON-LD):** Boost your rich snippets in Google search results. Automatically generate compliant schema for Articles, Breadcrumbs, and deep integration with WooCommerce Product Schema.
* **Automated XML Sitemaps:** Help search engines discover and crawl your content efficiently. Our sitemap generator handles posts, pages, categories, and products flawlessly. Includes StockTracker integration for WooCommerce to prioritize in-stock items.
* **Smart Redirects Manager:** Never lose link juice again. Easily create and manage 301, 302, and 307 redirects directly from the WordPress admin panel. Say goodbye to 404 errors and broken links.
* **IndexNow Integration:** Instantly notify major search engines (like Bing and Yandex) the second your content is created, updated, or deleted, drastically reducing indexing time.
* **Google Merchant Center (GMC) Ready:** Seamlessly connect your WooCommerce store with Google Merchant Center using our built-in API/REST Controller for optimized product listings.

### 🛒 Unmatched WooCommerce SEO Optimization

NovaTools - SEO goes beyond standard WordPress SEO. It is engineered from the ground up to maximize WooCommerce sales:
* **Product-Specific OpenGraph:** Ensure your products look stunning when shared on social media like Facebook, Twitter, and Pinterest.
* **Primary Category Selection:** Resolve duplicate content issues by setting a 'Primary Category' for products residing in multiple categories.
* **Advanced Product Filter SEO:** Gain control over faceted search. Our FilterDetector and Taxonomy `noindex` enforcer prevent search engines from crawling thousands of unoptimized, thin-content filter URLs.
* **Automated Image Alt Text:** Never miss an alt text again. Automatically generate fallbacks for product images to boost image search rankings.

Stop juggling multiple bloated plugins. NovaTools - SEO provides everything you need to optimize your WordPress site and WooCommerce store in one lightweight, incredibly fast package.

== Installation ==

1. Ensure you have the core **NovaTools** plugin installed and activated.
2. Upload the `novatools-seo` directory to your `/wp-content/plugins/` directory, or install it directly through the WordPress plugins screen via zip upload.
3. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' screen in WordPress.
4. Navigate to the NovaTools settings menu to configure your SEO preferences.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does this plugin work without the core NovaTools plugin? =
No. NovaTools - SEO is specifically built as an add-on and requires the core NovaTools plugin to be installed and active.

= Can I use this instead of Yoast or RankMath? =
Yes! NovaTools - SEO is designed to be a comprehensive, lightweight, and professional alternative to other major SEO plugins, with a special focus on deep WooCommerce integration.

= Does it automatically generate sitemaps? =
Yes, the plugin automatically creates and maintains an XML sitemap of your content to help search engines crawl your site.
